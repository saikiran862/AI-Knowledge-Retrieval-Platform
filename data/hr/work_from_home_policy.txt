1. HYBRID WORK MODEL
Associates whose roles are categorized as location-flexible are eligible to operate on a 3:2 hybrid schedule (3 days in designated corporate office, 2 days remote work from home). Full remote work requests require managerial and Vice President approval based on operational feasibility.

2. BROADBAND AND ERGONOMIC REIMBURSEMENT
To ensure a secure and productive home work environment, the company provides:
- Monthly Internet / Broadband Reimbursement: Up to INR 1,500 ($25 USD) per month against valid ISP invoices submitted through the expense portal.
- One-time Ergonomic Home Office Setup Allowance: Up to INR 15,000 ($200 USD) for acquiring an ergonomic office chair, desk, external monitor, and keyboard. Invoices must be submitted within 60 days of joining or transitioning to hybrid work.

3. DATA SECURITY AND VPN REQUIREMENTS
Employees accessing corporate repositories, client databases, or internal APIs while working remotely must connect exclusively via the corporate GlobalProtect VPN with multi-factor authentication (MFA). Using public unencrypted Wi-Fi networks without VPN is strictly prohibited.
