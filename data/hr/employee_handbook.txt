1. CODE OF BUSINESS ETHICS AND WORKPLACE RESPECT
Every associate at Global Enterprise Technologies is expected to uphold the highest standards of professional integrity, respect, and diversity. Zero tolerance is maintained toward discrimination, harassment, intimidation, or intellectual property theft.

2. CORE WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
Standard operational hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Monday through Friday. To support work-life harmony, core working hours are established between 10:30 AM and 4:00 PM local time, during which team collaboration, scrum standups, and cross-functional meetings should be scheduled. Employees must complete 40 working hours per week.

3. DRESS CODE GUIDELINES
The company promotes a 'Smart Casual' dress code from Monday through Thursday. Business formal attire is required when meeting external enterprise clients or attending regulatory boards. Casual wear is permitted on Fridays.

4. SEPARATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
Voluntary resignation requires a standard notice period of 60 calendar days for full-time employees, or 30 calendar days for probationary associates. A shorter notice period may be granted at the discretion of the Business Unit Head, subject to handover completion.
