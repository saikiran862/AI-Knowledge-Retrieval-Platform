1. ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT OVERVIEW
Full-time permanent associates are entitled to 24 days of paid time off per calendar year, structured into two distinct categories: Casual Leave (CL) and Earned Leave (EL/Privilege Leave).

2. CASUAL LEAVE (CL) VS. EARNED LEAVE (EL)
- Casual Leave (CL): 12 days per annum credited at the beginning of each calendar year. Casual leave is intended for urgent personal commitments or unforeseen illness of short duration (up to 3 consecutive days). Unutilized Casual Leave lapses at the end of the year and cannot be encashed or carried forward.
- Earned Leave (EL): 12 days per annum accrued at the rate of 1.0 day per completed month of active service. Earned Leave is intended for planned vacations, study leaves, or extended recuperation. A maximum of 45 days of accumulated Earned Leave may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year. Accumulated EL is encashable upon separation.

3. SICK LEAVE AND PARENTAL LEAVE
- Sick Leave (SL): 10 paid days per annum for certified medical illness. Medical certificates are mandatory for absences exceeding 2 consecutive working days.
- Maternity Leave: 26 continuous weeks of fully paid leave for female employees for up to two surviving children.
- Paternity Leave: 10 working days of fully paid leave to be availed within 6 months of childbirth or legal adoption.
- Bereavement Leave: Up to 5 consecutive paid working days in the unfortunate event of the loss of an immediate family member.
