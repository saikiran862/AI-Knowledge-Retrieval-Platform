1. GENERAL ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENT
All registered undergraduate and postgraduate students are required to maintain a minimum of 75% aggregate attendance across all registered academic theory and laboratory courses per semester to be eligible for the end-semester examinations.

2. CONDONATION OF ATTENDANCE DEFICIT
A student whose attendance falls between 65% and 74.9% may apply for attendance condonation on legitimate medical or extraordinary grounds. Applications for condonation must be submitted along with a certified medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner to the Office of the Dean of Academic Affairs within seven working days of returning to campus. A nominal condonation processing fee of INR 500 per subject is applicable.

3. ATTENDANCE BELOW 65%
Students with less than 65% attendance under any circumstances will not be permitted to appear for the end-semester examinations in that specific course and shall be awarded a 'W' (Withdrawn) grade. Such courses must be re-registered in the subsequent remedial or summer semester.

4. DUTY LEAVE (OD - ON DUTY)
Students officially representing the University in inter-collegiate sports tournaments, cultural events, hackathons, or academic conferences are entitled to an 'On Duty' attendance credit of up to a maximum of 10% total lecture hours, subject to prior approval by the Head of the Department (HOD).
