1. ENTRY AND IDENTIFICATION
Candidates must enter the examination hall at least 15 minutes prior to the commencement of the exam. A valid University Student Identity Card and the official Examination Hall Ticket must be visibly displayed on the desk throughout the duration of the test.

2. PROHIBITED ELECTRONIC ITEMS
Mobile phones, smartwatches, programmable calculators, Bluetooth devices, and any paper or study materials are strictly banned inside the examination hall. Possession of any electronic gadget, whether powered on or off, will be treated as an attempt to commit academic malpractice.

3. MALPRACTICE AND UNFAIR MEANS CODE
Any candidate found copying from notes, assisting other candidates, exchanging answer booklets, or utilizing unauthorized digital aids will have their paper confiscated immediately. The candidate will be handed over to the University Disciplinary Action Committee (UDAC). The standard penalty includes cancellation of all registered exams in the current semester and possible debarment for up to one academic year.

4. MINIMUM PASSING MARKS
To clear a course, a student must secure a minimum of 40% in the continuous internal assessment and at least 40% in the end-semester written examination, with an overall combined aggregate of 50%.
