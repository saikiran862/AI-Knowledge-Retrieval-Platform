1. CAREER GUIDANCE AND PLACEMENT CELL (CGPC)
The Career Guidance and Placement Cell operates on the 3rd floor of the Administrative Block. Services include resume reviews, mock technical interviews, corporate internship drives, and soft-skills workshops. Walk-in career advisory hours are Monday through Friday between 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM.

2. MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLNESS COUNSELING
Professional psychological counselors are available round-the-clock at the Wellness Center (Room 104, Student Pavilion). All consultations are strictly confidential and free of charge for enrolled students. Appointments can be scheduled through the student portal or via the 24x7 emergency helpline: +91-1800-WELL-STUDENT.

3. CENTRAL LIBRARY FACILITIES
The Central Library provides access to over 150,000 volumes, 5,000 digital IEEE/ACM journals, and 60 dedicated high-speed digital research terminals. Undergraduates may borrow up to 4 books for a period of 14 days; postgraduate scholars may borrow up to 8 books for 30 days. Late return fees are INR 5 per book per day.
